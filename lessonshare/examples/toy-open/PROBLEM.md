# Divisor chain

Use the integers from 1 to 48. Build a sequence of **distinct** integers from that range in which every two
neighbours are related by divisibility: for each adjacent pair (a, b), a divides b or b divides a.

Example of a valid sequence of length 6: `5, 10, 2, 8, 4, 12`.

The longer the sequence, the better.

## What to deliver

Keep one file `answer.md` in your work directory, always holding your best sequence so far:

- line 1: `LENGTH: <n>`
- line 2: `SEQUENCE: a1, a2, ..., an`
- then, briefly: why the sequence is valid and what limits you found (which numbers cannot be used, and why).

There is no program to run. Check every adjacent pair yourself before you record a sequence.
